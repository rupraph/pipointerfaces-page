    What is the pipo project ?

    Pipo Interfaces is a new family of modules designed to easily create interactions with music, lights, video, or 3D. Pipo modules turn movement, touch, distance, or other sensors into wireless MIDI or OSC controllers without coding.
    Pipo Interfaces is an open-source project aimed at spreading new tools to create unique and sensitive audiovisual experiences.
    Pipo will launch soon as pre-orders funding campaign on crowdsupply. You can visit the CrowdSupply campaign page and subsribe on the page to follow the progress.

        pipo motion

        A high precision wireless mouvement controller with 9 axis (orientation, acceleration and magnetometer). It allows low latency and sensitive motion sensing for MiDi and OSC, using absolute or relative orientation

          pipo analog

          A versatile I/O board to build anything in seconds. 8 voltage inputs (up to 12v) + 8 Touch inputs as MiDi/OSC/HID

            pipo range

            A distance sensing controller. Use it like a theremine (80 cm) or to sense approaching people or objects (up to 3m)

              Main features

              MIDI

              OSC (OpenSoundControl)

              Bluetooth

              WiFi

              USB-C

              No code, easy customization

                Each modules hosts a WiFi configuration page, to allow on the fly settings adjustment, from any device, without any application.

                  Project insights

                  Pipo is a project under developement. It is brought to you by RupR (Electronics Engineer) and supported by his friend Leo (Web Developper).

                  Pipo is an open source project, and contribution to the project are welcome. Feel free to write us.

                  Pipo development started more than a year ago, but its approach is the result of many years making interactive installations and missing the right tools. After years doing sensing with arduino and alternatives, I decided to build and share Pipo to enable people to go much further with realtime sensing.

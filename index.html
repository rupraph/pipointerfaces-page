Pipo Motion

Sense the movement

A high-precision wireless motion controller built around a 9-axis IMU (Accelerometer, Gyroscope, Magnetometer). Turn orientation, gestures, taps and vibrations into expressive MIDI or OSC

  • Absolute & relative orientation
  • Quaternion output over OSC
  • Low latency
  • Attach it anywhere

02

Pipo Analog

Build anything in seconds

A versatile input board for making custom interfaces or interactive objects using standard components. Plug switches, potentiometers, any kind of analog sensors, or turn any metal object into a touch trigger.

  • 8 voltage inputs - up to 12v - protected
  • 8 capacitive touch inputs
  • Selectable ranges
  • Solder pads for direct wiring

03

Pipo Range

A modern take on the theremin

A distance sensing controller powered by a high-performance Time-of-Flight sensor. Play it like a theremin with your hands, or use it as a proximity sensor to detect people and objects approaching.

  • Hand detection · up to 80 cm
  • Body detection · up to 3 m
  • Resistant to ambient light

Built-in config page

All settings on the fly

Every Pipo hosts its own graphical web interface you can access though Wi-Fi. Open it from any device, watch live sensor data, and tune everything in the field. No extra app required.

  • Adjust on the fly

    Tweak ranges, thresholds, inversion, outputs live — while you play.

  • Save multiple configs

    Keep a preset per project, venue or instrument and switch in one tap.

  • No app, no internet needed

    The UI is hosted on the module itself. Connect from any device with a browser.

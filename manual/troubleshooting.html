Pipo Troubleshooting

Tips

Questions about specs, compatibility or using several Pipos are answered in the FAQ.

Power & battery

Only the green LED is on, and I can't find the Pipo Wi-Fi

The battery is most likely too low. When the battery is too low, Pipo doesn't start: only the green ON LED lights up, with no Wi-Fi LED, no blinking and no Pipo Wi-Fi network. The green LED alone doesn't mean the battery has charge.

Switch Pipo off, charge it through USB-C, then switch it on again.

How do I know when charging is done?

The charging LED is red while charging and turns off when the battery is full (about 1h30 to 2h). While Pipo is running, the battery level is shown in the top right corner of the config page.

Config page

The config page reloads every 5–10 seconds

You probably have two browser tabs or windows open on the config page at the same time. Close all of them but one.

If you are using two Pipos of the same type, rename one of them first, otherwise both answer to the same address.

I can't open pipo-motion.local

  • Make sure you are connected to the Pipo's Wi-Fi (Pipo-Motion, Pipo-Analog... password pipo1234), or to the same network as the Pipo.
  • If your browser asks for permission to access your local network, accept it.
  • If you renamed your Pipo, its address changed too: it is now pipo-<new name>.local.
  • If no Pipo Wi-Fi network shows up at all, Pipo probably did not start: see the battery section above.

See Access the config page for the full steps.

The live values on the config page look choppy

That's expected: the live display on the config page doesn't reflect what is actually sent over OSC or MIDI. To check the real output, look at the data in your software, or in Protokol.

OSC data not arriving

It works with a direct connection, but not through my router

The most common cause: your computer's IP address changed when it switched from the Pipo's Wi-Fi to the router. Look up your computer's new IP address, enter it as the OSC destination in the Pipo config page, and save. You have to do this every time your computer changes network.

If it still doesn't work:

  • Use a router you own, with standard settings (a home router or a dedicated one). Public, guest, school and company networks often have security settings that block traffic between devices.
  • The UDP port set in Pipo (8000 by default) must not be blocked. On home routers it is usually open by default.
  • Make sure your computer's firewall lets your software receive incoming data.

The Data LED shows Pipo is sending, but my software receives nothing

  • Only one app can listen on a port at a time. Close any other app using the same port (including Protokol, if you used it to test), or set a different port in Pipo and in your software.
  • Check that the destination IP and port in Pipo match your computer and your software.
  • If your software lets you choose a network interface, select the one connected to the Pipo's network.
  • Remember that Pipo only sends data when an input changes: move or touch it.

Protokol is a quick way to check whether data reaches your computer at all. See the OSC page for the full setup.

Bluetooth MIDI

Pipo doesn't show up in my phone's Bluetooth settings

That's normal: Bluetooth MIDI devices don't appear in the phone's Bluetooth menu. Connect from inside your music app instead (not every app supports Bluetooth MIDI).

  • Mac: open Audio MIDI Setup, show the MIDI Studio window (Window menu), click the Bluetooth icon in the top right corner and connect Pipo.
  • Windows: a third-party Bluetooth MIDI app is required. For lower latency, prefer USB or OSC.

Also check that Pipo is in MIDI mode with Bluetooth enabled (settings panel, ). Bluetooth needs to be paired manually, while Wi-Fi reconnects automatically to a known router. See Bluetooth MIDI.

Pipo Motion data

My orientation values suddenly jump (from 180 to -180)

That's how Euler angles work: they wrap around at ±180° (and ±90°). Enable the Cyclic option on the channel to get an output without jumps (see channel settings), or use quaternions over OSC for robust 3D orientation.

Moving Pipo Motion up, down or sideways doesn't change any value

Pipo Motion measures orientation, rotation speed and acceleration, not its position in space. Linear acceleration only reacts at the start and end of a movement. To get a value that follows your gesture, tilt or rotate it instead. See Can Pipo Motion track its position? in the FAQ.

The yaw (heading) drifts

Small yaw drifts can occasionally happen, and the sensor recalibrates quickly. In relative mode, a short press on the function button captures a new zero orientation.

Firmware update

My settings are gone after an update

That's expected: a firmware update resets Pipo to factory settings, including saved configurations. Note your settings before updating. See Update Pipo.

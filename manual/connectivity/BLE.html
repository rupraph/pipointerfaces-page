Bluetooth LE MIDI Configuration

To connect Pipo using BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy):

  • on a smartphone: the connection is usually managed directly in the music or synth app (BLE will not show up in the normal Bluetooth menu of your phone)
  • on a Mac: go to "Audio Midi Setup" app. Then in Menu->Window, make sure to show the MIDI Studio window. In the MIDI studio, there is a Bluetooth icon on the top right. Click on it. Pipo should be listed for connection. When connected, pipo should appear as a normal Midi peripheral in your other softwares.
  • on PC, follow the normal bluetooth connection flow.

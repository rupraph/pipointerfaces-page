Getting Started with Pipo Modules

  • Pipo output modes can be changed to:

    • OSC over Wi-Fi
    • Midi over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
    • Midi over USB

  • Charge your battery before first use, or plug in power through the USB-C connector.

  • Pipo will not start if battery is too low (no other LED than ON are live). In this case, turn Pipo off and charge.

  • Charge indicator: the charging LED turns red when charging, and turns off when charging is complete.

  • There are 2 ways to connect with Pipo in Wi-Fi with a computer:

    • Direct connection: the computer connects to Pipo Wi-Fi access point
    • Standard connection: Pipo and the receiving computer are bot connected to same Wi-Fi router

  • Pipo only sends data when the input changes.

Pipo Analog – Sensor & Touch MIDI Interface

Pipo Analog board features:

  • 8 voltage inputs
  • 8 capacitive touch inputs
  • 4 voltage inputs have range selection (3.3, 5 or 12v range), the other 4 are 3.3v
  • All inputs have overvoltage and polarity inversion protection
  • Wifi/BLE
  • integrated battery
  • durable nylon (PA12) casing

Pipo Motion

Specs

Pipo motion is based around the high performance Inertial Measurement unit, ICM-20948 9-axis:

  • 3 axis accelerometer
  • 3 axis gyroscope
  • 3 axis magnetometer
  • ICM-20948 goes up to 400Hz refresh rate
